Each phase splits into reviewable PR-sized changes per PHASE_0, not one all-feature scaffold. Security/verified-email gates/audit timestamps belong to the phase introducing that activity; later hardening is verification, not first protection. No full outbox until Phase 7, enterprise capability engine, document management, malware pipeline, WebSocket, Redis, microservice or external search during Foundation. Legal retention durations/phone provider are not Foundation blockers.

Deferred register stays in PHASE_0 §15. Phase 3 resolves taxonomy/preferences/availability/completeness and discovery defaults. Phase 4 resolves pre-hiring Job preparation/coarse ads. Phase 5 resolves ordinary hiring lifecycle/capacity/material facts. Phase 6 resolves measured D1 skills/recurring overlap/coarse location, missing-data normalization, coverage, rounding/versioning, private bounded recommendations and minimal immutable apply facts. Future compensation/experience formula inputs, contact/address disclosure, moderation, invitations, retention, provider ops and analytics remain deferred. Phase 7 resolves its minimal D6 chat/terminal/block/read/polling/in-app decisions; invitations, email/preferences and asynchronous outbox remain deferred. Phase 8 resolves its D1 rating/reliability and D3 review visibility subset. Phase 9 resolves the minimal D5 subset recorded in PHASE_9; appeals/SLA/retention remain operational decisions. Phase 10 hardening is now authorized and recorded in PHASE_10. Stop after Phase 10; Phase 11 and deployment need separate authorization.
